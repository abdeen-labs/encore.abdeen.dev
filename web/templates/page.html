Encore

Text to speech, voice cloning, voice design, voice changing, multi-speaker scripts, and transcription, all on your own Mac. Nothing you type, record, or generate leaves your computer.

Clone

A voice from a few seconds of speech.

Drop in any recording or record a few sentences, trim it to the best 5 to 15 seconds, and save it. Encore transcribes the clip for the models that read along, and asks you to confirm the speaker's permission.

Design

Or describe one that doesn't exist yet.

Write what you hear in your head and audition several voices at once. Every audition is a different voice; keep the one you like and it joins your library.

Perform

Cast a whole scene.

Import a screenplay or a labeled script (.txt, .md, .fountain, or .pdf), cast each speaker, and perform it. Change one line and only that line is generated again.

Ladies and gentlemen, [pause 0.6s] tonight's performance begins.
[laugh] Please silence your phones.

[voice: Mira] Wait, has anyone seen the Captain?
  • Plain-text scriptsA few tags shape the performance: pauses, voice changes, and sounds like a laugh or a sigh, on the models that have them.
  • PronunciationsOne list fixes names, jargon, and acronyms for every voice.
  • FinishingLevel takes to a target loudness, tighten their silences, and export WAV, M4A, or MP3.

Change a voice.

Re-voice a recording in one of your voices while keeping its words, timing, and delivery. Switch between the original and the result to compare.

Transcribe.

Turn audio or video into text with sentence or word timings. Click a line to hear it, then export plain text, SRT, WebVTT, or JSON.

Keep a company of voices.

Cloned, designed, and imported voices live in one library with tags, notes, and their reference clips. Share one as an .encorevoice file.

Find every take.

Everything you make is kept with the script and settings it was made with. Search it, play it, export it, or send it back to the Studio.

Models

Sixteen models, downloaded as you need them.

From a 79 MB transcriber to 5 GB studio voices: Qwen3-TTS, VoxCPM2, Chatterbox, Zonos, Orpheus, Whisper, Parakeet, and more. Encore recommends the version that suits your Mac's memory, and runs each model where it runs best.

Models live in the standard Hugging Face cache, so other tools can share them. Each model's license is shown in the app.

Privacy

It stays on your Mac.

Nothing you type, record, or generate leaves your computer. There's no account and no cloud, and Encore collects no analytics, telemetry, or crash reports.

Encore goes online for two things only

  • Hugging FaceTo download the models you pick and, if you add a token for a gated model, to check that it works.
  • updates.abdeen.industriesTo check for a new version of Encore. It asks before checking automatically, and the check sends only Encore's version.

Free and open source.

Encore is a native Swift app built on Apple's MLX, with a bundled Python helper for the models that run best there. It's free under the MIT license; each model keeps its own license, shown before you download it.
